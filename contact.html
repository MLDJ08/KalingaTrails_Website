Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a hike?

Choose an available hike and use the booking option. You can complete the local demo booking workflow from the Trails & Hikes page.

What should I bring?

Bring water, snacks, comfortable hiking shoes, sun protection, and weather-appropriate gear.

How are completed hikes recorded?

Participation and completion are verified through the Kalinga Trails booking and guide-management workflow.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open My Bookings in your hiker dashboard to cancel a pending or confirmed request.

What do difficulty levels mean?

Easy is beginner-friendly, Moderate needs some endurance, and Challenging is suitable for experienced hikers.
