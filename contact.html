Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a hike?

Open Trails & Hikes, choose an available activity, and submit the booking form. Your request stays pending until the administrator confirms it.

Does submitting a request guarantee a slot?

No. The administrator must review the request and confirm the booking. Available slots and schedules are locally saved information in this browser.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. In this prototype, you can cancel a pending or confirmed request from My Bookings in your hiker dashboard. Bookings can be cancelled before the hike. Payments already recorded require an administrator to handle a refund outside the website.

How do payment records work?

Choose a payment preference when requesting a hike. Once your booking is approved, you may provide a transfer reference in My Bookings if you used GCash or bank transfer. An administrator records payments only after independently checking them. The website does not process transactions or send funds.

What should I bring?

Check your selected hike page for trail-specific guidance. Common essentials include water, snacks, suitable hiking shoes, rain protection, and any personal items you may need.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy is designed for beginners, Moderate involves more endurance, and Challenging is intended for experienced hikers. Review distance, duration, elevation, and current conditions before choosing.

How do hiking achievements work?

After a booked activity, the assigned guide checks attendance and records completion. Eligible verified completions update the customer's hiking history and achievements.

Booking & Service Policies

Proposed business rules for the fictional company. Only the actions described as available in the prototype are implemented.

Requests & confirmation

A booking request does not equal a confirmed reservation. An administrator reviews participant and scheduling information before confirmation.

Cancellations & changes

Hikers can cancel pending and confirmed bookings in their dashboards. A cancelled booking that has a recorded payment is flagged for refund review. Refunds must be arranged outside this website.

Package inclusions & payment

Each scheduled hike shows a starting price per person. The booking page calculates an estimated total and lets hikers choose pay-on-site, GCash or bank transfer as a preference. After confirmation, hikers may submit a transfer reference for administrator review. Administrators can record that payment has been independently verified. No money is collected or refunded through this website; all prices and policies are illustrative.

Safety & weather

Participants must review difficulty, gear guidance, and the meeting location. A future operating policy should allow the organizer to postpone or cancel unsafe hikes and contact affected customers.
