About Kalinga Trails

Care for the trail. Care for the community.

Kalinga Trails is a proposed hiking and outdoor adventure business focused on organized and guided hiking experiences.

Our Story

A hiking experience built around people

Kalinga Trails connects customers with hiking guides and a community of fellow hikers. The name “Kalinga” reflects the idea of care and support.

Our platform brings together hiking discovery, booking, guided-hike management, social interaction, achievements, and long-term hiking progress.

What Guides Us

Mission, vision, and values

Mission

To provide organized and meaningful hiking experiences while building a supportive community that encourages people to explore trails, develop their interest in hiking, and celebrate their progress.

Vision

To become a trusted hiking community and guided outdoor adventure provider that encourages more people to explore mountains and trails through accessible, organized, and engaging hiking experiences.

Core Values

Safety, care, community, and respect for nature guide the way Kalinga Trails approaches hiking and customer experiences.

Our Operating Approach

Care from preparation to completion

A guided hike involves more than reaching the summit. The customer, guide, and administrator each have a clear role.

Before the hike

Customers review the trail difficulty, schedule, meeting place, what to bring, and safety reminders before requesting a booking.

During the hike

The assigned guide checks participants in, shares safety reminders, and records attendance. Hikers follow the guide and local trail rules.

After the hike

The guide records completion. Verified hikes then contribute to eligible customers’ hiking history and achievements.
